# Demo script

The README opens with `docs/demo.gif`, a silent loop of about 45 seconds. This page is how to record a fuller demo by hand: a silent screen recording of about 85 seconds (1:25), ending on the Help page. It uses only the app's bundled example, which shows a fictional library model. No real person appears, and no real photo is used.

## Before you record

- Serve the built site: `npm run build`, then `npm run preview`, and open `http://localhost:4180/huecut/`.
- Use a fresh browser profile with no saved session. Otherwise the home page offers **Continue where you left off** instead of the usual buttons.
- Use a desktop window of about 1280 x 800, the light theme, and motion left on (Settings, Motion: Follow my device).
- The example needs no photo and no download of the face tools.
- Before you start, save two or three JPEG pictures of the same fictional model, one for each look you will add on **My pictures**. They stand in for pictures from a chat. Do not open ChatGPT or Gemini, and show no account.
- Keep the mouse slow. Pause about a second on each result.

## Script

Times are where each beat starts. The labels are the ones on screen.

| Time | Do | Show |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00 | Open the home page. Scroll slowly past the first panel, the face lines drawing, the wall of looks and the colour cards. | The promise "Find a cut and colour that suit your hair, and know why." Then the colour cards with their labels: Plausible, Uncertain, Best done by a colourist. |
| 0:08 | At the bottom, click **See an example first**. | The step opens on **Your face card**, with the "Example" badge and "On a fictional model". |
| 0:12 | Scroll down the face card slowly. | The photo with its lines, then the four facts (Length and width, Forehead, Nose to chin, Jaw), each with a number marked as an estimate. |
| 0:20 | Click **Move the lines**. Drag the chin dot a little, then click **Done**. | The numbers change as the dot moves. |
| 0:27 | Under **More with your photo**, click **Try a colour on your photo**. Tap two looks. Close it. | The hair changes on the photo. The label "Rough preview" is visible. |
| 0:35 | Click **Choose what to change**. On Your goal, point at Both and Women's styles, then click **Continue**. | The goal is already chosen in the example. |
| 0:39 | On the questions, answer one or two quickly. If a "Tap the looks you like" screen appears, tap two pictures. Then click **Skip to my ideas**. | Pictures and drawings as answers. "Not sure" is always the last option. |
| 0:46 | On Your ideas, scroll slowly. Pause on a cut with its "Because" lines, then on a colour idea with its label. | Cuts and Colours, the "Works with your hair today" line and the colour label. Point at **Colour in your results** and **Let ChatGPT or Gemini pick your best 3**, without clicking them. |
| 0:56 | Click **Show your stylist** on one idea. Scroll the sheet a little. Close it. | The sheet a stylist can read at once. |
| 1:01 | Click **Try them on**. On **All together**, scroll to the prompt and the reference sheet. Do not click Copy and open. | The finished-card prompt and the numbered reference sheet. |
| 1:06 | Click the **My pictures** tab. Add your prepared picture to one look with **Add picture**. Click **See your pictures side by side**. | The picture appears in its slot, then on the board with "AI picture, an illustration of the look" under it. |
| 1:12 | On the board, tap the tile and drag the slider between your photo and the AI picture. | The comparison slider. |
| 1:17 | Open **Help**, then **Privacy**. | "The app never uploads your photos." The line on MediaPipe's usage reports, which the app blocks. |

## After recording

- Check every frame for anything personal: a name, a file path, a tab title or a notification.
- Every picture must be of the fictional model.
- Export it as an animated GIF, with no audio and no metadata that names your tools or your computer.
- If a button label has changed since this script was written, change the script to match the screen.
